09 — Defensibility

Cloning a rocket with AI is hard — hardware and flight heritage

Hardware you can't download

Engines, cryogenic test stands, a launch site, and a qualified supply chain take years and hundreds of millions to build. No model compresses a static-fire campaign or a recovered booster into software.

Flight heritage & licensing

Customers and regulators only trust proven flights. FAA launch licenses, range approvals, and a mission track record accrue one launch at a time — and can't be simulated into existence.
